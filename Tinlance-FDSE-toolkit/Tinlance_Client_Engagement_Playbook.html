1
Use Zoom / Google Meet / WhatsApp — your choice
Do NOT use phone. Screen sharing matters. When they ask about the dashboard, you share your screen and show it live. Opens http://13.50.16.19/dashboard right in front of them.

Ask these questions — in this order
Listen 70% of the time. Take notes. The answers tell you which service to propose.
❓

❓

❓

❓

❓
Do you have a budget allocated for security this quarter?
❓

Identify which service they need
Based on their answers, one of these will be obvious:
AI Deployment Sprint
  • They want to deploy AI capabilities inside their stack
  • LLM integration, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows

Security Engineering Embed
  • They need detection, hardening, or compliance proof
  • ThreatFade + BugFlow + Report + NIS2/DORA mapping
  • 2–6 weeks, $6,000–$15,000

Most common for you right now: Security Engineering Embed. Your Nigerian fintech contacts and EU NIS2 clients fall here. This is what ThreatFade + BugFlow + the full toolkit was built for.

Phase 6 · Day 7–18 · The actual engagement
Deployment + detection + assessment

This is the week-by-week breakdown of what you do, what runs on your VPS, and what (optionally) runs on their machine.

Week 1 — Reconnaissance + External Assessment
1
Run ReconOS OSINT scan — on YOUR VPS
Map their entire public attack surface. DNS records, subdomains, exposed services, certificate data, employee LinkedIn profiles, GitHub repositories.
Your VPS — ReconOS + Identity Scanner # Full identity threat scan
python3 identity_scanner.py --domain client.com --out ./engagements/client_name

# ReconOS OSINT fusion (if available)
python3 reconos/main.py --target client.com --out ./engagements/client_name
2
Run BugFlow Elite scan — on YOUR VPS
Automated vulnerability discovery against their in-scope external IP addresses and web applications. BugFlow runs on your Contabo VPS at port 8502. Results come back as JSON.
Your VPS — BugFlow Elite v6 (Contabo port 8502) # Open BugFlow dashboard in browser
http://185.252.232.253:8502

# Or CLI — run scan against their external IPs
python3 bugflow/main.py --target client-ip-range \
--scope "portal.client.com,api.client.com" \
--out ./engagements/client_name/bugflow_results.json
🔒
Everything stays on your VPS. BugFlow sends probes TO the client's systems, but the engine, results, and all data are processed and stored on your Contabo VPS only.
Week 2 — Network Detection + Internal Assessment
3
Deploy ThreatFade — 2 options depending on client type
Option A — Remote (most common)
  • Client exports PCAP file from their network
  • They send it to you via secure link
  • You run ThreatFade on your VPS against their PCAP
  • Nothing gets installed on their machine
  • Best for: most clients, remote engagements
Option B — Agent deployed (deeper scan)
  • You send them ThreatFade endpoint agent (compiled binary)
  • They run it on ONE machine — it captures traffic locally
  • Results JSON sent back to you via secure link
  • You process on your VPS
  • Best for: on-site, air-gapped, or deep engagement
Option A — Your VPS — analyse their PCAP # They send you: client_traffic.pcap
# You run on your VPS:
python3 threatfade/main.py \
--pcap ./received/client_traffic.pcap \
--siem json \
--out ./engagements/client_name/threatfade_results.json

# Also accessible via FusionOps API:
curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8001/detect/scenario \
-F "pcap=@./received/client_traffic.pcap"
Option B — What you send to client machine (one file only) # Send them TinlanceIDScan binary via remote delivery portal
python3 remote_delivery.py \
--package ./binaries/TinlanceIDScan \
--client "Their Name" \
--out ./delivery_package

# They run on their machine:
./TinlanceIDScan --domain theirdomain.com --out ./results

# They send results.json back to you
# You process everything on your VPS
4
Monitor FusionOps dashboard during the engagement
Open your SOC console. This is your live command centre for the whole engagement.
Your live dashboard — keep this open during engagement http://13.50.16.19/dashboard

# When ThreatFade processes their PCAP, detections appear here
# You see: severity, z-score, affected host, MITRE TTP
# Screenshots of this become evidence in the final report
📸
Take screenshots of every detection. When ThreatFade fires an alert, screenshot the FusionOps dashboard. These screenshots become the "Evidence" section in each finding in the final report. Clients love seeing the actual detection screen.

If the client is EU-based or under NIS2/DORA, run the compliance gap assessment. This adds significant value and justifies the higher price tier.

# Phase 10 — Regulatory Intelligence

Regulatory claims are now represented as source-backed assessments rather than static prose. Each assessment records regime, jurisdiction, statement type, applicability status, official source, source title, effective date, requirement topic, and optionally a deadline/authority only after the FDSE has established those facts for the engagement.

Maintained official references currently include the EU NIS2 Directive and DORA Regulation. The Toolkit does not assert customer applicability automatically and does not hard-code notification deadlines into generic playbooks.

Legal requirements, regulator guidance, and Tinlance recommendations are distinct statement types. Customer-specific legal applicability remains a legal/compliance review decision.

# Phase 9 — Incident Response Playbook Engine

The historical playbooks were audited and found to contain hard-coded regulatory statements and fixed notification timing. The maintained generator removes legal conclusions from the template and requires an engagement-specific regulatory assessment.

Playbooks cover C2, credential theft, lateral movement, ransomware, supply-chain compromise, and AI-agent abuse. Each output includes containment, evidence preservation, communications, regulatory assessment, recovery, and closure sections.

Regulatory text is deliberately framed as an assessment task. The FDSE must record the applicable jurisdiction, authoritative source, effective date, competent authority, and deadline rather than relying on a static template claim.
